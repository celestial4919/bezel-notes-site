Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED — NOVEMBER 06, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Celestial Forge, owned and operated by Ogwang Joseph Peter, handles information in connection with the Bezel Notes website and software.

01. Information We Collect

We aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary to operate, support, license, and improve Bezel Notes.

Website

The Bezel Notes website does not intentionally request personal information from you through ordinary page browsing. Our website provides product information and links to checkout and other services.

Purchases

Purchases are processed through a third-party payment service provider. That provider may collect and process information such as your name, email address, billing information, payment information, purchase details, IP address, and other information necessary to process transactions, prevent fraud, provide customer support, and comply with legal obligations.

We may receive limited purchase-related information, such as your email address, transaction status, and purchase reference, where necessary to fulfil your order, issue your software license, or provide customer support.

The payment provider processes personal information according to its own privacy policy and applicable legal requirements.

Licensing and Device Information

Bezel Notes uses a licensing service to validate licenses and manage permitted device activations. When the application is activated, it may process your license key and a device fingerprint or other device identifier for the purpose of validating the license and enforcing the applicable machine limit.

The licensing service used by Bezel Notes is Keygen.

02. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes including:

Processing and fulfilling purchases; providing access to Bezel Notes; validating software licenses; managing authorized device activations; providing customer support; preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized use; maintaining and improving the software; and complying with legal obligations.

03. Your Notes and User Content

Notes and other content you create in Bezel Notes belong to you.

Bezel Notes is designed to store your notes locally on your device. We do not intentionally collect or sell the text of your notes through the Bezel Notes website or ordinary use of the application.

You are responsible for maintaining backups of important information stored in the application.

04. Third-Party Services

Bezel Notes relies on third-party services for specific functions. These may include:

Payment Service Provider — payment processing, transaction management, fraud prevention, and payment-related customer support.

Keygen — software license validation and machine activation management.

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

05. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may receive or share limited information with service providers where necessary to process purchases, fulfil software licenses, provide support, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

06. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including fulfilling purchases, maintaining licensing records, resolving disputes, providing support, and meeting legal or accounting obligations.

07. Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

08. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights relating to your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing.

Requests may be submitted using the contact information below. Where a third-party service such as Paddle is responsible for particular personal data processing, you may also need to exercise your rights directly with that provider.
